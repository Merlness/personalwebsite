Delete Your Enki Account

App: Enki (Enki Voice), by Bennu Systems LLC

Contact: privacy@bennusystems.com

You can delete your Enki account and its data at any time. There are two ways.

Fastest: delete inside the app

  1. Open Enki.
  2. Go to Settings > Privacy & account.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Deletion takes effect immediately.

No access to the app? Request deletion by email

Email privacy@bennusystems.com with the subject "Enki account deletion request" from the email address associated with your account (or describe the account so we can locate it). We verify the request and complete the deletion, confirming by reply. We respond within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and its authentication records.
  • Stored CRM connection credentials.
  • Captures, proposed records, spreadsheet rows, and usage records in Enki's active production database.
  • In-app deletion also clears Enki's local capture history, digital-card profile, onboarding state, and saved credentials from that device.

What deletion cannot remove

  • Records you already approved and saved to a CRM, a downloaded spreadsheet file, or your phone contacts. Delete those copies at their destination.
  • Deleted information may remain temporarily in encrypted backups until the normal backup rotation completes (up to 30 days). We may retain narrowly limited records longer when required by law, to resolve disputes, or to prevent fraud or abuse.
