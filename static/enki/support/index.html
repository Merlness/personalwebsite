Common questions

Do my business-card photos or voice recordings get uploaded?
No. Text recognition and voice transcription happen on your device. Your photos and recordings never leave your phone, and we don't keep them.

Which CRMs does Enki work with?
Pipedrive, HubSpot, Salesforce, and Zoho, with more rolling out. No CRM? Save to a spreadsheet with zero setup.

Does anything save automatically?
No. You review every contact, note, and follow-up before anything is saved.

How do I delete my data?
Open Enki and choose Privacy & account > Delete account. That removes your account and its data from our systems and clears local data from your device.
