Common questions

Do my business-card photos or voice recordings get uploaded?
Your business-card photos are never uploaded. Enki reads the text on your phone and sends only that text. Voice depends on your phone: on iPhone, your voice never leaves the device. On Android, Enki uses a third-party voice-to-text service, so your recorded audio is sent to that service to turn it into text. Enki does not keep the audio afterward, and the voice-to-text service is configured not to keep it either. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Which CRMs does Enki work with?
Pipedrive, HubSpot, Salesforce, and Zoho, with more rolling out. No CRM? Save to a spreadsheet with zero setup.

Does anything save automatically?
No. You review every contact, note, and follow-up before anything is saved.

How do I delete my data?
Open Enki and choose Privacy & account > Delete account. That removes your account and its data from our systems and clears local data from your device.
